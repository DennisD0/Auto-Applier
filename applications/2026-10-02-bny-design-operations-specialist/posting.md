## Job description

We are seeking a Design Operations - Specialist for our Design Practice. The role will be
located in our New York, NY offices.

Candidate will support the orchestration and optimization of the people, processes, and
practices necessary to foster a creative environment for a dynamic design practice. In this
role, candidates will need to demonstrate initiative and program management skills to support
and help develop best practices for the UX team on how we work together, and how we get work
done.

The candidate will help establish the operational foundation, strategy, and direction to ensure
the UX teams have what they need to thrive and deliver great work aligned with strategic
initiatives. This includes facilitating internal meetings, coordinating with head of design ops
on scheduling/HR/team health/budget, identifying pain points in internal processes, and
supporting solutions, all while fostering a collaborative and inclusive culture.

This role will need to prioritize amongst deadlines, and shifting priorities. Always with the
underlying goal of helping us as a team grow and develop new skills, while increasing trust,
transparency, and engagement.

Candidate should be adept at utilizing data to articulate their rationale, are a clear
communicator, and explain recommendations with confidence and openness. Must maintain both
drive and patience throughout the journey.

Candidate must build trust and engage cross functional partners in UX, Product, Tech & Business
stakeholders to set the stage for the team to achieve exceptional outcomes. Should be curious,
ask questions, and be driven to connect the dots.

## Essential requirements

- Experience in Project Management/Operations role.
- Experience working in complex, matrixed, global or multi-national organizations
- Proactively identify issues that may impair the team from meeting strategic, business, or financial goals
- Strong data-driven visual, verbal, and written story-telling skills
- Track record in using a strategic approach to solve complicated problems and strong collaboration skills
- Strong communication skills

## Desirable skills

Working knowledge of the financial services industry, security markets, and regulations a plus.

## Education

Bachelor's degree in psychology, user experience, design, human computer interaction or a
related discipline, or equivalent work experience required, advanced degree preferred.
