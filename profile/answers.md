## Never fill — abort and flag instead

If a form requests any of the following, I stop that application and hand it to you:

- Social Security Number
- Date of birth
- Driver's license number
- Bank account / routing / payment details
- Any password or account credential typed into a page field
- Background-check or credit-check consent
- A signature (typed or drawn)
- Document upload of a passport, ID, or visa
